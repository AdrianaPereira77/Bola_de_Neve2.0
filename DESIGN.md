## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Response
The design system delivers an empathetic, authoritative, and empowering financial sanctuary. Tailored for users transitioning from acute debt anxiety to active wealth creation, the visual language departs from sterile banking spreadsheets and predatory fintech dashboards. It balances institutional composure with deep psychological safety ("holding the user's hand"). The emotional arc moves from relief and emotional de-escalation to clarity, intentionality, and pride of progress.

### Design Movement
**Sophisticated Atmospheric Glassmorphism & Bioluminescent Dark Mode.**
The foundation is built upon deep, velvety obsidian and slate tones layered with controlled volumetric light sources. Semi-translucent glass membranes with micro-borders isolate data without generating visual noise. Gamified moments (debt snowball milestones, surplus allocations) evoke tactile reward through restrained, bioluminescent highlights rather than juvenile graphics.

## Colors

### Semantic Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#10B981` — Emerald Catalyst):** Represents wealth velocity, debt eradication, and capital compounding. Used for affirmative actions, snowball progress bars, and net worth growth.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` — Electric Cyan):** Dedicated to the AI Financial Mentor and analytical intelligence. Signifies foresight, algorithmic guidance, and predictive simulations.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` — Warm Amber):** Reserved for tithes, ethical stewardship, spiritual generosity, and milestone unlockables. Infuses human warmth and purposeful allocation.
- **Support Emotional Tier (`#8B5CF6` — Serene Violet):** Anchors psychological equilibrium, behavioral coaching, financial anxiety de-escalation, and mindfulness checks.
- **Emergency Tier (`#F43F5E` — Soft Coral Rose):** Applied strictly to high-interest debt warnings, predatory credit flags, and the "SOS Superendividamento" emergency flow. Replaces alarming crimson with an urgent yet non-punitive tone.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#020617` — Deep Slate 950):** The deep obsidian baseline, paired with slate step-downs (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`, `#334155`) to construct optical depth without flat grey fatigue.

## Typography

### Structural Decisions
- **Editorial & Structural Voice (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its geometric structure softened by open apertures and humane curves. It removes the bureaucratic coldness typical of traditional banking apps while projecting clarity.
- **Financial & Quantitative Voice (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to all currency figures, interest rates, countdown timers, and snowball debt payback milestones. The fixed character width enforces optical tabular alignment, eliminating visual jitter during live balance recalculations.
- **Case Hierarchy:** All uppercase treatments are strictly confined to `label-caps` in JetBrains Mono to introduce an architectural, audit-grade feel across data tags, loan category markers, and tracker headers.

## Layout & Spacing

### Mobile-First Fluid Grid Rhythm
The system operates on an 8-point base module (scaled down to 4px for tight internal card metrics). The mobile envelope prioritizes ergonomic reachability within the primary thumb zone (bottom 60% of viewport).

- **Mobile Canvas (<640px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutter. Content sections rely on edge-padded vertical stacking with floating contextual utility bars.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile Fold (640px–1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Snowball simulation charts unlock horizontal comparison matrices with the mentor dialog pane.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Data-dense rows use `space-sm` (8px) internal padding, whereas cognitive breathing areas (such as mental health and coaching modules) utilize `space-xl` (32px) margins to ease cognitive load.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Glass Layering
Depth is produced via light refraction, backdrop diffusion, and ambient glow instead of heavy drop shadows.

- **Base Void (Layer 0):** Pure Slate-950 (`#020617`), solid backdrop.
- **Surface Membrane (Layer 1):** Slate-900 at 75% opacity (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an interior hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`. Used for passive list cards and secondary metrics.
- **Interactive Container (Layer 2):** Slate-800 at 80% opacity (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.80)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a top edge highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Hero Focus & Floating Action Sheets (Layer 3):** Semi-opaque surfaces backdropped with colored radial spotlights (e.g., a faint 10% Emerald glow for progress dashboards, or a 10% Cyan glow for AI prompts).
- **Hairline Contours:** Shadows are grounded by crisp `1px` translucent outer boundaries (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06), 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`).

## Shapes

### Rounded-2xl Foundation
A uniform radius of `1rem` (16px) defines default structural components, expanding to `1.5rem` (24px, or `rounded-2xl`) for primary hero cards, modal sheets, and the debt-payoff accelerator widget. Pill formats are dedicated to quick-tap category chips, financial tag indicators, and contextual AI prompts. The balance of wide radii with micro-borders produces a tactile, gem-like visual feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Snowball Accelerators:** Emerald base (`#10B981`) with rich dark slate typography (`#020617`, weight 700). High contrast, subtle inner top shadow, and an active spring scale-down (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Mentor Intelligence Actions:** Cyan tinted glass (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`), cyan border (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`), electric cyan label.
- **SOS Emergency Trigger:** Desaturated coral rose surface with white text, triggering an unhurried, reassuring multi-step intake flow.

### Financial Cards & Snowball Modules
- Glass surfaces with `1px` gradient borders running from semi-opaque white at the top to transparent at the bottom.
- Visual hierarchy: Currency values rendered in high-contrast `numeric-hero` JetBrains Mono, paired with a semantic colored sparkline or micro-progress bar.
- Interactive states feature dynamic bioluminescent border glows matching the card's domain (Emerald, Cyan, Violet, or Amber).

### Input Fields & Monetary Keypads
- Grounded in recessed containers (`rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.8)`), featuring a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Active focus smoothly transitions the border to Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`) with a soft 3px cyan focus halo.
- Numeric inputs automatically surface dedicated currency symbols (`R$`) in muted mono caps alongside inline interest-rate indicators.

### AI Mentor Dialogue Bubbles
- Positioned as asymmetrical glass pills (`rounded-2xl` with a sharp bottom-left anchor).
- Left-aligned indicator strip colored in Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`) denoting automated insights, paired with instant actionable suggestion pills below.

### Emotional Check-in & SOS Sheets
- Modal views surfaced on Violet-tinted charcoal backgrounds with smooth drag handles.
- Non-judgmental tone, open spacing, soft interactive slider controls, and zero high-contrast red alerts to prevent financial avoidance behavior.
